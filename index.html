Hello, I'm Philipp

Environmental Scientist and birding enthusiast.

I've always been fascinated by nature, when I was a child I always wanted to be a field researcher. I studied Biology and Environmental Sciences, afterwards got my M.Sc. in Geophysics. Nowadays I work as a consultant for groundwater remediation and try to save our drinking water. In my free time I like to look at birds, plants and other animals and once a year I will try to do the same abroad.

I mainly want to use this page to share some of my favorite stories and maybe encourage others to explore the world on their own.
